What Elevate does

Elevate: Business Planner is a daily planner for founders and creators. It combines a to-do list, habits, goals, a content calendar, revenue and write-off tracking, and optional wellbeing tracking in one app for iPhone.

Elevate and your Google account

You can sign in with Google, and optionally connect Google Calendar (to show your events and add the important dates you create) and Google Sheets and Drive (to send the write-offs and revenue you choose to a spreadsheet in your own Google account). Elevate uses this data only to provide these features. Read exactly how in the Google user data section of our Privacy Policy.
