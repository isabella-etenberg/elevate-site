Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Elevate ("Elevate", "we", "us") is operated by Isabella Etenberg Holdings Inc. in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what information the Elevate app collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: your information is yours, we use it only to run the app for you, and we never sell it.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, a display name you choose, and your password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form).
  • Things you add to the app: tasks, habits, goals, notes, important dates, content-planning items, and the settings you choose.
  • About you, from setup: when you first open Elevate we ask for the name you'd like to be called, your birthday, whether you are a founder, creator, or both, your industry, the features you want to use most, your biggest challenges, and your monthly revenue goal. We use these to personalize the app and to understand what people need. You can change your name and birthday in the app's settings.
  • Money entries: revenue and write-off entries you log, such as amounts, descriptions, store names, dates, currencies, and categories, plus any receipt or proof photos you attach.
  • Wellbeing entries: if you use the health and cycle features, the cycle dates, moods, symptoms, and other entries you choose to log. This is optional and entirely up to you.
  • Photos: images you add to your vision board or outfit tracker, or attach to entries. The app only accesses your camera or photo library when you ask it to, and only the photos you select.

Calendars and other accounts you connect

  • Apple Calendar: if you allow it, Elevate reads and adds events on your device's calendar. This stays on your device.
  • Google Calendar and Outlook: if you connect them, Elevate reads and writes events through your own sign-in with Google or Microsoft. Your sign-in credentials stay with Google or Microsoft and on your device; Elevate does not receive your Google or Microsoft password.
  • Google Sheets: if you connect it, Elevate writes your chosen entries to a spreadsheet in your own Google account, and may copy attached receipt images to your Google Drive so the sheet can link to them.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features and keep your data in sync and backed up between sessions.
  • To send account emails you trigger, such as email confirmation and password reset.
  • To respond when you contact support.
  • To understand, in aggregate, what features and challenges matter to our customers so we can improve Elevate and how we describe it.

We do not use your personal information to show you other companies' ads, we do not sell or rent it, and the app contains no third-party advertising trackers.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage, and sign-in system. Your data is stored in Canada (Central).
  • Apple processes subscription payments; we never see your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat confirms whether your subscription is active so we can unlock Premium. It receives an app user ID and your purchase information.
  • Resend delivers our account emails (it sees your email address and the message being sent).
  • Google, Microsoft, and Apple only if you choose to connect their calendar or spreadsheet features, as described above.
  • Currency conversion: when you log an entry in a foreign currency, the app asks a public exchange-rate service (Frankfurter) for the rate. Only the currency codes and the date are sent, never your account or amounts.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How your data is protected

Data is encrypted in transit. Each account's data is kept separate using row-level security, so one user's information cannot be read by another. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (menu, then Account, then Delete account). Deleting your account permanently removes your account, your entries, and the photos and receipts you uploaded. Copies in routine backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your information in the app, or ask us to help. Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, or the GDPR in the EU and UK), you may have additional rights such as obtaining a copy of your data or objecting to certain processing. Contact us and we will respond promptly.

Children

Elevate is for people aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. If you are under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission to subscribe.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app or by email.
